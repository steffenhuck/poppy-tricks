Poppy's First Tricks

Poppy does all sorts of things by herself. She sniffs, she barks, she spins, she jumps. And every now and then, she sits.

That's your moment: press YES! She gets a treat, and she'll do it more often. Her thought cloud shows what's on her mind: the bigger the word, the more likely she does it next.

How puppies learn tricks

  • Wait for it. You can't tell a puppy what to do. Poppy tries things all by herself. When she does the trick, press YES! straight away.
  • The treat goes to whatever she's doing right now. A treat while she barks teaches her to bark. A treat after she's stopped is only half a lesson.
  • What pays, grows. Watch her thought cloud: rewarded ideas get bigger, ignored ones shrink.
  • One thing leads to another. A paw only comes after a sit. A roll only comes after lying on her side.
  • Hold back to get more. Once she knows a step, stop rewarding it. She'll try harder, and do a bit more.
  • Words come last. Say "Down!" just as she lies down, then YES! Do it a few times and the word means something.
  • The old way: push her into it. She ends up sitting, but she learns nothing, and she trusts you a little less.

Each well-timed YES! for the trick fills a paw at the top. Fill them all and she's learned it. Treats are limited, and so is time: about two minutes per trick. If she hasn't learned the trick when the treats or the time run out, it's game over, and you start again from trick 1. Only the last trick, rolling over, is forgiven: the show goes on without it.
